## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes a large **Swiggy food dataset containing 197,430 records** covering the period from **January 2025 to August 2025**.

The goal is to transform raw food-delivery data into meaningful business insights using **Microsoft Excel**, including KPI analysis, monthly trends, food-type comparisons, restaurant analysis, pricing analysis, and geographic exploration.

The project demonstrates practical skills in **data cleaning, data analysis, Pivot Tables, KPI calculation, and business-focused data visualization**.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis focuses on answering practical business questions:

- What is the overall sales value and average order value?
- How does sales performance change month by month?
- What is the sales contribution of Veg vs Non-Veg food?
- Which restaurants and locations contribute most to sales?
- Which food categories and dishes are popular?
- How do food prices vary across locations and categories?
- What is the overall customer rating performance?
- How does rating volume vary between restaurants and food items?
